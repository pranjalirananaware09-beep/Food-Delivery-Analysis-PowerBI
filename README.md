Comprehensive Food Delivery & Restaurant Operations Dashboard
Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) Focus: Goal 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth) & Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption & Production)

📌 Project Overview
This Power BI project provides a granular analysis of a food delivery ecosystem involving 560+ unique orders. The dashboard is designed to help restaurant owners and logistics managers track sales performance, payment trends, and delivery efficiency to drive data-backed decision-making.  

📊 Business Problem & Domain Relevance
In the competitive food-tech industry, high cancellation rates lead to significant revenue leakage and food waste. This project addresses these challenges by:  

Analyzing the delivery success rate (currently 93.8%) to minimize operational losses.  

Identifying top-performing menu items and restaurant branches (IDs 211 & 217).  

Monitoring the shift toward digital payments (UPI vs. COD) to understand customer behavior.

🌿 SDG Mapping

SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production): By tracking and identifying the causes of the 6.2% cancellation rate, this dashboard helps restaurants reduce food waste.  


SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth): Supporting local restaurant growth through sales optimization and digital payment adoption.  

🛠️ Data Cleaning & Transformation (CO1)
The following preprocessing steps were applied to the raw dataset to ensure 100% accuracy:  

Type Conversion: Standardized orderDate and Time formats for accurate time-intelligence analysis.

Handling Missing Values: Cleaned null entries in the delivery status and payment method columns.

Deduplication: Removed duplicate order_id entries to ensure the integrity of the total order count (563 unique orders).

Formatting: Applied consistent naming conventions to all food categories and restaurant IDs.

📈 Key Features & Interactivity (CO4)

Executive Overview: High-level KPI cards for Total Orders and Quantities.  


Dynamic Slicers: Interactive filters for Restaurant ID and Date ranges to allow branch-specific analysis.  


Advanced Visuals: Use of Funnel Charts for delivery drop-offs and Treemaps for menu popularity.  

Page Navigation: Seamless movement between Sales, Customer, and Operational views.
